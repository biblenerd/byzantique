---
anchor: MAL 3:1-3
title: Purifying the Temple priesthood
tags: prophecy, translation
---

Malachi prophesies that the Messiah's coming will involve His appearance at the Temple (v. 1), and the purpose of His coming to the Temple is to purify its priesthood.

The "fire of a furnace" is a clear indication of a refiner's fire (*cf.* [v. 3](ref:MAL 3:3)), but "herb of fullers" is unclear as a translation of ποία πλυνόντων (*poia plynontōn*). Brenton has a footnote that the Greek indicates "them that wash," and more recent translations indicate this is some form of cleansing, perhaps launderer's soap.[^fn1]

[^fn1]: NETS has "lye of cleaners."
