---
anchor: HEB 12:2
title: Is it "our" faith?
tags: translation
---

The KJV and [TCENT](/about/texts-and-translations/#tcent) both insert "our" before "faith" into this passage, but there is no direct support for that in the underlying Greek text. 

Fr. Pat Reardon notes:

>There is no manuscript support for the insertion of this modifier, and it fits ill with the large historical sweep of the author's view of faith. Hebrews is concerned about faith&mdash;or perhaps *the* faith&mdash;and not just *our* faith. On the contrary, faith permeates the whole of salvation history. It does this, moreover, as a principle of continuity, because "without faith it is impossible to please" God ([Heb. 11:6](ref:HEB 11:6)).[^reardon]

[^reardon]: Patrick Henry Reardon, *Reclaiming the Atonement: An Orthodox Theology of Redemption: The Incarnate Word*, vol. 1 (Chesterton, IN: Ancient Faith Publishing, 2015), 40.
