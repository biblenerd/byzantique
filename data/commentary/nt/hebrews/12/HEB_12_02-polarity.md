Fr. Pat Reardon points out how "author and perfecter" create a polarity based on their root lemmata:

>*Archegos* [ἀρχηγός] ("author") is based on the root *arche* [ἀρχή], which means "beginning," and *teleotes* [τελειωτής] ("perfecter") is derived from *telos* [τέλος], which means "end." "Beginning" and "end" are syntactical poles. Thus, as the two nouns are employed in this text&mdash;covered by a single article in Greek&mdash;they convey the tension of contrast. Jesus is both the beginning and the goal of faith.[^r2]

[^r2]: Patrick Henry Reardon, *Reclaiming the Atonement: An Orthodox Theology of Redemption: The Incarnate Word*, vol. 1 (Chesterton, IN: Ancient Faith Publishing, 2015), 40.
