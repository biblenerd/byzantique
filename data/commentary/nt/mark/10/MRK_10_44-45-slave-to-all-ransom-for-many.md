title: Slave to all... ransom for many
tags: translation, typology
---

>The phrase for many is an Aramaic expression meaning "for all."[^fn1]

διακονῆσαι (*diakonēsai*) is likely associated with [Isaiah 52:13](ref:ISA 52:13) and [53:11](ref:ISA 53:11) (also δουλεύοντα πολλοῖς / *douleuonta pollois*) (LXX), thus alluding to the Suffering Servant.[^fn2] "But Mark 10:45 is not a translation of any portion of [Isa 52:13–53:12](ref:ISA 52:13-53:12)...; it is a summary of the task of the Servant."[^fn3]

[^fn1]: Seyoon Kim and F. F. Bruce, *1 & 2 Thessalonians*, ed. David B. Capes, Second Edition, vol. 45, Word Biblical Commentary (Grand Rapids, MI: Zondervan Academic, 2023), 219; Jack Norman Sparks, *The Orthodox Study Bible: Notes* (Thomas Nelson, 2008), 1346.

[^fn2]: Craig A. Evans, *Mark 8:27–16:20*, vol. 34B, Word Biblical Commentary (Dallas: Word, Incorporated, 2001), 120–121.

[^fn3]: *Ibid.*, 121.
