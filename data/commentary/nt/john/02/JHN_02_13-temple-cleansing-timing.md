St. John places the cleansing of the Temple prior to the *first* Passover of Jesus' public ministry, whereas the Synoptics portray it prior to the *final* Passover of His life (*cf.* [Matthew 21:12-17](ref:MAT 21:12-17); [Mark 11:15-19](ref:MRK 11:15-19); [Luke 19:45-48](ref:LUK 19:45-48)).
