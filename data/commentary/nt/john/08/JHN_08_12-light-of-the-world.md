Jesus continues the theme in the Temple of connecting Himself to the sunlight being prayed for in [the Festival of Booths (also known as "the Feast of Lights")](note:JHN_07_02-festival-of-booths), which continues [the water/light theme of this festival](note:JHN_07_37-39-water-and-light).[^fn1]

>Jesus claims to replace the rain ([7:37-38](ref:JHN 7:37-38)) and sunlight (8:12) for which Israel prayed.[^fn2]

This is further connected to the original association of the festival with the wilderness wanderings of ancient Israel. 
* Water => water from rock in wilderness (*cf.* [1 Corinthians 10:4](ref:1CO 10:4)).
* Light => pillar of cloud and fire representing God's presence "tabernacling" among his people in Jesus (*cf.* [John 1:14](ref:JHN 1:14) and [Exodus 40:34](ref:EXO 40:34)).
