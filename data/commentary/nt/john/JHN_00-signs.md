There are seven miracles which St. John refers to as "signs" (σημεῖα / *sēmeia*) in his Gospel account:

1. Changing water to wine at wedding in Cana ([2:1-11](ref:JHN 2:1-11), *cf.* [v. 11](ref:JHN 2:11))
2. Healing an official's son ([4:46-54](ref:JHN 4:46-54); *cf.* [v. 54](ref:JHN 4:54))
3. Healing the paralytic at the pool of Bethesda ([5:2-15](ref:JHN 5:2-15))
4. Feeding the 5,000 ([6:1-14](ref:JHN 6:1-14); *cf.* [v. 14](ref:JHN 6:14))
5. Walking on the water ([6:15-21](ref:JHN 6:15-21))
6. Healing the man born blind ([9](ref:JHN 9); *cf.* [v. 16](ref:JHN 9:16))
7. Raising Lazarus from the dead ([11:1-44](ref:JHN 11:1-44); *cf.* [12:17-18](ref:JHN 12:17-18))
