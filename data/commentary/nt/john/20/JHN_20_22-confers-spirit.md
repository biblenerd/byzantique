Jesus is "the one who baptizes with the Holy Spirit" ([John 1:33](ref:JHN 1:33)).

>John's Gospel portrays Jesus as the One who confers the Spirit.... The Holy Spirit is the gift breathed forth upon the Church from the risen Body of Christ.[^reardon]

[^reardon]: Patrick Henry Reardon, *Reclaiming the Atonement: An Orthodox Theology of Redemption: The Incarnate Word*, vol. 1 (Chesterton, IN: Ancient Faith Publishing, 2015), 51.
