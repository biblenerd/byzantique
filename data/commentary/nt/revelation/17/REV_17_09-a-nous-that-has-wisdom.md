anchor: REV 17:9
title: A nous that has wisdom
---

>Ὧδε ὁ **νοῦς** ὁ ἔχων σοφίαν.[^fn1]

Apocalyptic literature calls for a *nous* that has wisdom.

>... apocalyptic imagery and language readily lend themselves to fanaticism and unbridled speculation. As we see in contemporary examples of preaching and publication, this danger is real, but it is hardly insuperable. Understanding and critical discernment are required. The most apocalyptic book of the New Testament testifies that apocalyptic images require special discernment....[^fn2]

[^fn1]: [GRCBYZ](/about/texts-and-translations/#grcbyz), Revelation 17:9 (emphasis added). Transliteration: "*hŌde ho **nous** ho echōn sophian.*"

[^fn2]: Patrick Henry Reardon, *Reclaiming the Atonement: An Orthodox Theology of Redemption: The Incarnate Word*, vol. 1 (Chesterton, IN: Ancient Faith Publishing, 2015), 279.
