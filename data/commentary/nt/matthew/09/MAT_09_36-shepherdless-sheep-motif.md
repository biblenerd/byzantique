anchor: MAT 9:36
title: Shepherdless sheep motif
---

St. Matthew calls to mind a number of Scriptural allusions to πρόβατα μὴ ἔχοντα ποιμένα (*probata mē echonta poimena* = "sheep without a shepherd") or similar, as does St. Mark in the parallel in [Mark 6:34](ref:MRK 6:34), where the Good Shepherd imagery from [Psalm 22(23)](ref:PSA 22) is [connected to the Eucharist](note:MRK_06_34-44-the-good-shepherd-feeds-his-people-in-the-wilderness).

- [Numbers 27:17](ref:NUM 27:17) &mdash; πρόβατα οἷς οὐκ ἔστι ποιμήν (*probata hois ouk esti poimēn*) == "sheep without a shepherd"
- [3 Kingdoms (1 Kings) 22:17](ref:1KI 22:17) &mdash; ποίμνιον ᾧ οὐκ ἔστι ποιμήν (*poimnion hō ouk esti poimēn*) == "a flock without a shepherd"
- [2 Supplements (Chronicles) 18:16](ref:2CH 18:16) &mdash; πρόβατα οἷς οὐκ ἔστι ποιμήν (*probata hois ouk esti poimēn*) == "sheep without a shepherd"
- [Judith 11:19](ref:JDT 11:19) &mdash; πρόβατα οἷς οὐκ ἔστιν ποιμήν (*probata hois ouk estin poimēn*) == "sheep for whom there is no shepherd"
- [Zechariah 10:2](ref:ZEC 10:2) &mdash; ἐξηράνθησαν ὡς πρόβατα (*exēranthēsan hōs probata*) == "they have withered like sheep"
- [Ezekiel 34:5](ref:EZK 34:5) &mdash; διεσπάρη τὰ πρόβατά μου, διὰ τὸ μὴ εἶναι ποιμένας (*diesparē ta probata mou, dia to mē einai poimenas*) == "my sheep were scattered, because there were no shepherds"

*See also:*

- <a href="https://www.pseudepigrapha.com/pseudepigrapha/2Baruch.html#C77.13" target="_blank">2 Baruch 77:13</a> &mdash; "the shepherds of Israel have perished"[^fn1]

>The evangelist has already asserted, on the basis of OT texts, that the Messiah will 'shepherd' Israel ([2:6](ref:MAT 2:6)), and there is some evidence that ‘shepherd’ carried messianic connotations in Judaism ([Jer 3:15](ref:JER 3:15); [23:4](ref:JER 23:4); [Ezek 34:23–4](ref:EZK 34:23-24); [37:24](ref:EZK 37:24); <a href="https://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/nets/edition/31-pssal-nets.pdf" target="_blank">Ps. Sol. 17:40</a>; <a href="https://www.sefaria.org/Midrash_Tehillim.29.1?lang=bi&with=Translations&lang2=en" target="_blank">*Midr. Ps.* on 29:1</a>). Is not Jesus the messianic shepherd, whose responsibility it is to gather eschatological Israel?
>
>Is there also a Mosaic typology in the background of 9:36? Moses was a shepherd ([Exod 3:1](ref:EXO 3:1); Philo, *Vit. Mos.* 1:60–6;[^fn3] Josephus, *Ant.* 2:263–4;[^fn4] LAB 19:3, 9[^fn5]); and in [Num 27:17](ref:NUM 27:17) it is the departure of Moses which occasions the concern that Israel might become 'like sheep which have no shepherd'. When one also recalls that there are certainly places in Matthew where Jesus is one like Moses and that some Jews no doubt expected the last redeemer (Messiah) to be like the first redeemer (Moses), the reader should perhaps think that Jesus the shepherd is taking up a Mosaic office when he seeks out the lost sheep of the house of Israel.[^fn2]
