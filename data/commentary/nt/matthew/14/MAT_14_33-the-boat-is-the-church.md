---
anchor: MAT 14:33
title: The boat is the Church
tags: typology
---

>Matthew makes an explicit point of the fact that the confession of faith took place "in the boat," a detail rather superfluous unless there is a special symbolic meaning in the boat. We are surely correct in seeing the confessing Church symbolized in Peter's apostolic fishing boat.[^fn1]

[^fn1]: Patrick Henry Reardon, *Reclaiming the Atonement: An Orthodox Theology of Redemption: The Incarnate Word*, vol. 1 (Chesterton, IN: Ancient Faith Publishing, 2015), 231.
