This is similar to Peter's confession in [16:16](ref:MAT 16:16) (perhaps linking these accounts or forming an *inclusio* of what lies between).

It is also notable they *prostrated* (προσήνεγκαν / *prosēnenkan*) before Jesus (translated here as "worshiped him"). This is same posture the disciples were later in when receiving the Great Commission (*cf.* [28:17-20](ref:MAT 28:17-20)). *Cf.* [note on prostration / adoration in Matthew](note:MAT_00-prostration-or-adoration).
