anchor: MAT 21:11
title: THE Prophet like Moses
tags: translation
---

The definite article is important here. The crowds are saying Jesus is *the* Prophet (ὁ προφήτης / *ho prophētēs*; i.e., the prophet like Moses, who was prophesied in [Deuteronomy 18:15](ref:DEU 18:15)). St. John the Forerunner was also asked about this ([John 1:21](ref:JHN 1:21)). The identification of Jesus as this prophet is also part of St. Peter's preaching in [Acts 3:22](ref:ACT 3:22).
