---
anchor: MAT 17:6
title: The disciples fell on their faces
tags: transfiguration
---

Amongst the parallel accounts of the Transfiguration ([Mark 9:2-10](ref:MRK 9:2-10); [Luke 9:28-36](ref:LUK 9:28-36)), only St. Matthew records that the disciples "fell on their faces," perhaps carrying on [the motif of prostration before Jesus](note:MAT_00-prostration-or-adoration).
