title: Connections to Exodus 24
tags: typology, transfiguration
---

The "six days" likely ties this account to Moses' ascent of Mt. Sinai in [Exodus 24](ref:EXO 24) (*cf.* especially [24:16](ref:EXO 24:16)). [There are a number of other parallels](note:MRK_09_02-six-days-from-mt-sinai-to-mt-tabor), and the Orthodox Church appoints portions of [Exodus 24](ref:EXO 24) and [33](ref:EXO 33) to be read at Vigil for the Transfiguration.
