---
anchor: MAT 26:25
title: Only Judas Iscariot calls Jesus "Rabbi" in Matthew
---

St. Matthew avoids calling Jesus "Rabbi" when compared to the Gospels of Sts. Mark and John.[^fn1] The only person to do so in St. Matthew's Gospel is Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Him (here and at His betrayal in [v. 49](ref:MAT 26:49)).

[^fn1]: *Cf. also* [23:6-8](ref:MAT 23:6-8).
