Just as fish are drawn out of water, so also people are brought into the boat (or ark) of the Church through baptism.[^fn1]

[^fn1]: This thought was inspired by St. Clement of Alexandria, *The Instructor* (*Paedagogus*), 3.11, ANF, vol. 2, pp. 285-286 (which can be read online at https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/anf02/anf02.vi.iii.iii.xi.html ). Clement was speaking about the types of images acceptable on Christians’ seals, and said, "and if there is a person fishing [on the seal], he [the owner of the seal] will remember the apostles, and the children drawn out of the water [of baptism]."
