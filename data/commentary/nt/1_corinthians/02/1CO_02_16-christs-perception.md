title: Christ's perception
tags: translation
---

Fr. Pat Reardon proposed that a better translation of νοῦν Χριστοῦ (*noon Christou* / "mind of Christ") is "Christ's perception," which conforms more with the Latin Vulgate's *sensus Christi*.

Extrapolating from this, Reardon contends that the apostles learned to understand the things of God the way that Christ Himself understands them.[^reardon]

[^reardon]: Patrick Henry Reardon, *Reclaiming the Atonement: An Orthodox Theology of Redemption. Vol 1: The Incarnation*, vol. 1 (Chesterton, IN: Ancient Faith Publishing, 2015), 21.
