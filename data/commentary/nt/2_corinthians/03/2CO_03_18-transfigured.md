title: Transfigured

The word translated "transformed" is μεταμορφούμεθα (*metamorphoumetha*), the same lemma used for Jesus' Transfiguration.[^fn1] I think a better translation is "transfigured," to make this connection clearer.

[^fn1]: *Cf.* [Matthew 17:2](ref:MAT 17:2) and [Mark 9:2](ref:MRK 9:2). It also appears in [Romans 12:2](ref:ROM 12:2).
