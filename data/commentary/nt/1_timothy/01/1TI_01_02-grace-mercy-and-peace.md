title: Grace, mercy, and peace
---

Another variation of St. Paul's greeting is "grace, mercy, and peace" which is seen in the Pastoral Epistles (*here* and in [2 Timothy 1:2](ref:2TI 1:2); [Titus 1:4](ref:TIT 1:4)) and also in [2 John 1:3](ref:2JN 1:3).

*Cf. also* [note on the "grace and peace" greeting](note:ROM_01_07-grace-to-you-and-peace) in general, and how it intentionally addresses both a Gentile and Jewish audience.
