Early Christians associated the Transfiguration with [the Feast of Tabernacles / Booths (Sukkot)](note:JHN_07_02-festival-of-booths) because of the mention of erecting shelters in this passage.[^fn1]

[^fn1]: Parallels in [Matthew 17:4](ref:MAT 17:4) and [Mark 9:5](ref:MRK 9:5).
