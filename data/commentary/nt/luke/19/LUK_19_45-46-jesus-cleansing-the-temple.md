title: Jesus cleansing the Temple
tags: prophecy
---

The Holy Prophet Malachi prophesied that the Messiah would appear in the Temple[^fn1] and purify its priesthood (*cf.* [Malachi 3:1–3](note:MAL_03_01-03-temple-priesthood)).

[^fn1]: The Temple is an important theme in St. Luke's Gospel, which [begins](note:LUK_01_05-09-temple) and [ends](note:LUK_24_52-53-temple) in the Temple.
